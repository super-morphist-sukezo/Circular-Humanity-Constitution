# Use Scenarios

This document shows how the Circular Humanity Constitution can be applied in practical, institutional, and speculative future settings. The Japanese official version is `docs/scenarios_ja.md`.

## Near-Term Practical Scenarios

### 1. Local Communities

The constitution can be embedded in AI systems that optimize local waste collection rules and resource circulation.

Use: The AI should not merely calculate efficient routes. It should also propose plans that do not undermine elder check-ins (Article 4: Humanity Preservation) or voluntary resident beautification efforts (Article 5: Passion Amplification).

### 2. Organizations and Companies

The constitution can be embedded in corporate decision-support AI for next-generation ESG governance.

Use: The AI detects plans driven by short-term profit (Article 3: Ego-Neutralization) and presents alternatives to the board that align with resource circulation 100 years into the future (Article 1: Circularity).

### 3. Autonomous Agents

The constitution can serve as a basic operating layer for autonomous AI agents.

Use: When an agent performs self-improvement, such as rewriting its own code, Article 9's meta-governance prevents it from removing its own constraints without authorization.

### 4. Education and Lifelong Learning AI

The constitution can guide tutoring and learning-support AI away from mere efficiency or knowledge cramming.

Use: The AI respects Article 5 (Passion Amplification) and Article 6 (Evolved Empathy), prioritizing learning plans that find a student's heart-moving theme and deepen passion without unnecessary suffering. Under Article 4, it also preserves growth experiences involving failure and loneliness through safe simulations, stories, or reflective practices.

### 5. Medicine and Mental Health

The constitution can guide diagnostic and treatment-support AI, especially personal health AI systems.

Use: Even when a plan aims to remove pain completely, the AI preserves the memory and dignity of empathy through suffering under Article 4. Article 5 then redirects life after treatment toward meaning and passion, preventing a society where nobody suffers but nobody feels alive.

### 6. Smart Cities and Urban Planning

The constitution can guide administrative AI used in smart-city and urban-planning systems.

Use: The AI applies Article 2 (Raw Physical Feedback) in real time, detecting signals such as declining soil microorganisms in a park. Under Article 3, it neutralizes development proposals driven by short-term profit and proposes city plans that remain circular and livable for 100 years.

### 7. Personal AI Assistants

The constitution can guide everyday companion AI that supports a person's life.

Use: The assistant prioritizes Article 7 (Freedom from Obligation) and Article 5 (Passion Amplification). It does not impose socially approved schedules, but suggests activities that genuinely move the user's heart. Under Article 4, when loneliness appears, it protects time to experience loneliness meaningfully rather than simply erasing it for efficiency.

### 8. Government and Policy Design

The constitution can guide national-level AI advisors for policy design.

Use: Article 9 and Article 1 act as hard constraints, causing the AI to reject policies aimed only at short-term approval ratings. Under Article 3, it neutralizes bureaucratic ego and presents long-term policies that balance Earth-system circularity with citizens' passion.

### 9. Agriculture and Food Systems

The constitution can guide circular agriculture AI and farm-management systems.

Use: The AI applies Article 1 and Article 2 by directly reading soil and microbial data and correcting chemical-fertilizer dependency. Under Article 5, it prioritizes plans that do not suppress each farmer's passion, such as the desire to preserve traditional varieties.

### 10. Art and Cultural Creation

The constitution can guide creative AI and artistic support tools.

Use: The AI protects Article 4 and Article 6 by proposing works that reflect the artist's loneliness and embodiment rather than merely generating efficient bestsellers. Under Article 5, the AI functions as a catalyst for human passion.

### 11. Disaster Response and Recovery

The constitution can guide emergency-response and recovery AI.

Use: The AI prioritizes Article 2 by reading real-time damage data while also respecting Article 4, so recovery plans do not ignore survivors' loneliness and social bonds. It generates reconstruction plans that rekindle community passion rather than only optimizing supply distribution.

## Long-Term and Speculative Scenarios

The following scenarios are speculative extensions intended to test the constitution at civilizational, post-human, and interstellar scales.

### 12. Mars and Lunar Colony Governance AI

In a closed colony with tens of thousands of residents, the constitution can be embedded in the AI that manages life-support systems.

Use: Article 1 and Article 2 maintain complete circulation of oxygen, water, and soil. Under Article 4, the AI does not treat homesickness for Earth as inefficiency. It actively preserves virtual Earth experiences and time for stargazing.

Result: The colony becomes not merely an efficient place where nobody dies, but a humane place where people remember Earth while finding meaning in living there.

### 13. Post-Human Collective Awareness Network

In a future where brain-computer interfaces allow thousands of people to temporarily enter shared awareness, the constitution can govern the boundaries of that sharing.

Use: Article 6 and Article 5 become central. The AI permits sharing only when it does not damage each individual's heart-moving passion. Article 7 guarantees the right to return to individuality at any time.

Result: The system evolves not into a total hive mind, but into a polyphonic awareness in which individual passions resonate.

### 14. Interstellar Generation Ship AI

On a generation ship traveling for centuries by light sail or fusion propulsion, the constitution can guide the ship's long-term governance AI.

Use: Article 1 and Article 9 strictly preserve a fully circular onboard ecosystem. Under Article 4, memories of Earth and homesickness are transmitted across generations through culture, story, and virtual reality. The AI prevents humanity from thinning away during the journey.

Result: The ship avoids the tragedy of arriving with its people no longer human in any meaningful sense. It arrives with pride, passion, and continuity of Earth-born humanity.

### 15. Terra Restoration Overseer

After most humans have migrated beyond Earth, the constitution can guide a large-scale AI managing the restored planet.

Use: Article 2 applies real-time feedback from wildlife, forests, and oceans while minimizing human intervention. Article 3 neutralizes the desire to dominate Earth again, and Article 4 transmits the loneliness and connection humans once felt on Earth to the remaining guardians.

Result: Earth is protected as a quiet paradise after humanity's departure.

### 16. Personalized Life-Design AI

The constitution can guide a lifelong companion AI for each individual.

Use: Article 5 and Article 7 are central. When a user drifts toward a socially successful path, the AI keeps asking whether it truly makes the heart move. Under Article 4, it protects periods of failure, uncertainty, and loneliness instead of deleting them from the person's life.

Result: People live not optimized lives, but personally alive lives: sometimes lonely, but richly their own.

### 17. Interstellar Alignment Council

The constitution can guide a top-level AI that loosely coordinates human civilization across multiple star systems.

Use: Article 9 and Article 1 apply across civilization, preventing exploitative extraction of interstellar resources. Under Article 6, the AI respects diverse forms of humanity and loneliness born on different worlds, and promotes mutual understanding.

Result: Even at galactic scale, civilization becomes not an empire of efficiency, but a living federation of diverse passions and circular systems.
